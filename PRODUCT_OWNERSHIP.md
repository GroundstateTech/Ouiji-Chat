# Ouiji Chat Product Ownership

Ouiji Chat is an official product of **Groundstate Technology LLC**.

## Groundstate-owned product assets

Groundstate Technology LLC controls the official Ouiji product identity, including:

- the Ouiji and Ouiji Chat names and official branding;
- logos, official artwork and release presentation;
- the official repository and release channels;
- official binaries, installers and code-signing identities;
- Groundstate-operated Ouiji servers and hosted services;
- product domains, service accounts and operational infrastructure;
- the official roadmap and compatibility policy;
- copyrights owned or assigned to Groundstate Technology LLC.

## Source code license versus product identity

The repository's software license grants rights in covered source code. It does not transfer ownership of the official product or grant permission to impersonate Groundstate or an official Ouiji release.

Where the software license permits forks, modified distributions must preserve required license notices and use distinct branding unless Groundstate Technology LLC grants written permission to use official branding.

## Community development

Groundstate welcomes community participation. Contributions accepted after adoption of `CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md` are accepted under that agreement so the official product can maintain coherent copyright ownership and licensing authority.

Contributing does not grant equity, ownership of Groundstate Technology LLC, ownership of the Ouiji product, control of the official roadmap, or rights to official branding.

## Deployment model

Groundstate intends Ouiji to support both:

- a Groundstate-operated public internet service; and
- independently operated private servers for businesses, organizations, labs, communities and closed networks.

Running an independent server does not make that deployment an official Groundstate-operated service. Operators are responsible for their own infrastructure, security, privacy practices and legal obligations.

## Additional licensing

Groundstate may offer additional commercial, enterprise, hosted-service or private-deployment terms in the future. Community source licensing and separate commercial arrangements may coexist where Groundstate has the necessary rights.

This document describes project policy and is not a substitute for the repository software license or a signed commercial agreement.
